📖 AK SimLink Lite Firmware Update Guide
⚠ IMPORTANT

AK SimLink Lite cannot automatically detect the installed firmware version. Please check your current firmware version in Windows before updating.

Step 1 — Check Your Current Firmware Version

  1. Plug your AK SimLink Lite into your PC normally.
  2. Press Win + R, type joy.cpl, then press Enter.
  3. Open the controller properties.
  4. The firmware version is shown in the controller name.

    Example:

    AK SimLink Lite v1.5.1
  5. Compare your current version with the firmware versions listed below. Only update if a newer version is available.

Step 2 — Enter Bootloader Mode

  1. Disconnect the USB cable.
  2. Press and hold the BOOT button.
  3. While holding the button, plug the USB cable into your PC.
  4. Release the BOOT button after the USB is connected.

Step 3 — Install Firmware

  1. Select the firmware version below.
  2. Click the INSTALL button.
  3. Select the COM port.
  4. Wait until the installation finishes.
  5. Do not disconnect the USB cable during the update.

Step 4 — Restart and Verify

  1. Unplug and reconnect AK SimLink Lite normally.
  2. Open joy.cpl again and confirm the firmware version has changed.
✔ Update Complete

Reconnect your AK SimLink Lite normally and open joy.cpl again to verify that the firmware version has been updated successfully.

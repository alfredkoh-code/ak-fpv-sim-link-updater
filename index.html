WHY WE CREATED IT.

The closest simulator experience to real FPV flying.

Every FPV pilot wants simulator practice to feel as close as possible to real flying. But today's options all come with compromises.

Bluetooth adapters provide wireless freedom, but they introduce additional latency and consume more transmitter battery. Traditional USB cables offer a direct connection, but they limit movement, create cable clutter, and keep you tethered to your computer.

AK SimLink was designed to solve both problems. By communicating through your own FPV receiver and your own radio transmitter, it preserves the same control path you use during actual flights. The result is a wireless simulator experience with authentic stick feel, real receiver response, and the closest possible connection to real-world flying.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for setup, compatibility, firmware updates, and product differences.

Which AK SimLink model should I choose?

Choose AK SimLink Lite if you only need ELRS simulator support. Choose AK SimLink Pro if you want multi-protocol support such as ELRS, Crossfire, FrSky, onboard screen, menu system, and future advanced features.

Does AK SimLink need drivers?

No special driver is required. AK SimLink appears as a standard USB HID gamepad on supported systems.

Which simulators are supported?

AK SimLink is designed to work with simulators that support standard USB gamepad input, including popular FPV simulators such as Liftoff, VelociDrone, Uncrashed, DRL Simulator, FPV SkyDive, and TRYP FPV.

Can I update firmware myself?

Yes. Firmware can be updated directly from the browser using the AK SimLink firmware updater. Lite and Pro have separate updater pages to prevent installing the wrong firmware.

Does Lite support Crossfire or FrSky?

No. AK SimLink Lite is designed as a simple ELRS simulator adapter. For Crossfire and FrSky support, choose AK SimLink Pro.

What should I do if the device is not detected?

Try a different USB cable, use a direct USB port on the PC, avoid USB hubs during firmware updates, and confirm that your cable supports data transfer. Some USB cables are charging-only.
